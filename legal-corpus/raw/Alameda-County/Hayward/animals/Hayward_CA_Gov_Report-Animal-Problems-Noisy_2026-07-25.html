Report animal problems (noisy, wild, stray, dead)

Most animal-related noise complaints are a result of the owner not being aware of the problem, outside influences causing the animal to make noise, or a lack of training. In the majority of complaints the owner is willing to work with neighbors to resolve the problem.

We highly recommended that you first approach the owner of a noisy animal and discuss the problem with them directly. Due to limited resources, Animal Control Officers cannot always respond quickly to every complaint and will not respond to anonymous complaints.

To report a noisy animal, call 510.293.7200 with the following information:

  • Your name, address, and phone number
  • Address of the animal
  • Times at which the animal is making noise
