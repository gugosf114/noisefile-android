Barking dogs and animal bites

To report a dog bite, or to file a barking complaint, please call the shelter at 510-535-4884.

If a public safety risk exists (i.e. an aggressive dog at large) or a stray animal needs emergency assistance, please call the police non-emergency line at 510-777-3333.
