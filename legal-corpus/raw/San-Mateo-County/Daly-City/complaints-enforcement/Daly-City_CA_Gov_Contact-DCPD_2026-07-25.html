Contact DCPD

The Daly City Police Department is located at City Hall - 2nd floor

Daly City Police Department
333 90th St.
Daly City, CA 94015

Front Desk Hours:

  • Monday through Friday: 8:00 a.m. - 6:00 p.m.
  • Saturday: 8:00 a.m. - 12:00 p.m.
  • Sundays & Holidays: CLOSED

Phone Numbers:

  • Emergency: 9-1-1
  • 24/7 direct dispatch and after hours: (650) 992-1225
  • Daly City Police Department Front Desk - Non-Emergency (650) 991-8119


Note: Dialing 9-1-1 from your cellular telephone will send you to the California Highway Patrol in Vallejo. We are currently working on technology that will enable us to receive cellular 9-1-1 calls. Until that technology has been refined and is in place, we are asking that you dial (650) 991-8119 from your cellular telephone when reporting non-emergency incidents in the City of Daly City.

EMERGENCY
CALL 9-1-1
