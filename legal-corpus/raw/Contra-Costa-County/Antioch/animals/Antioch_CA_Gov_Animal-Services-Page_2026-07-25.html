Animal Services

Volunteer Opportunities

Anyone wanting to volunteer for Antioch Animal Services should visit our volunteer website, create a volunteer profile, and attend an upcoming volunteer orientation. Volunteer Orientations are held on the second Saturday of every month at 9 am and the second Wednesday of every month at 7 pm in the Police Department Community Room.

Sign up!

Animal Services is dedicated to promoting animal welfare and public awareness, which results in humans and animals living together in harmony.

The Antioch Animal Services Center is responsible for the enforcement of local and state laws relating to the care, control, and protection of animals and of our citizens. Officers' responsibilities include picking up and confining stray, sick, injured, vicious and dead animals; issuance of dog licenses; patrolling the City streets; control of animal nuisance problems and animal euthanasia. The Animal Shelter also provides adoption, volunteer, and education programs.

All the animals are housed indoors in a heated/air-conditioned facility.

Special features include:

  • A room in which prospective pet owners can meet and interact with the animals
  • Two fenced exercise yards

Additional Information 

Microchips for dogs and cats - $25, includes microchip implant, registration on the Animal Shelter database, and unlimited updates for the first year through PetLink.

Pet owners may also receive lifetime updates directly with PetLink by paying a one-time $9.99 registration fee to PetLink. Information about PetLink will be emailed directly to you after your microchip is implanted and registered.

A late fee of $34 will be added when applicable.

For additional information or to purchase a pet license, call Antioch's Pet Licensing service at 877-238-2698 or visit the PetData website.

    Contact Animal Services

    300 L Street Antioch CA 94509
    Directions

    Phone: 925-779-6989

    City of Antioch residents only – all others contact Contra Costa County. 

    Hours

    Tuesday through Saturday
    10 am to 5 pm

    Closed: Sunday, Monday, all City Observed Holidays and any Tuesday that follows a holiday.

    We are open to the public for adoptions, redemptions, purchase of a city dog license, microchip services and donation drop-offs. Although appointments are not necessary, they will be given priority.

    Call 925-779-6989 from 8:30 am to 5 pm; phones will be answered on days of business.

    After Hours

    Animal-related emergencies can be directed to the Antioch Police Department non-emergency line at 925-778-2441. An animal emergency is for example: aggressive, injured or sick animals. If your own pet is injured or sick, please seek veterinary assistance. The local 24-hour emergency vet is located at 1214 Sunset Dr, Antioch and can be reached at 925-754-5001.

    Please call 911 if a person or animal is getting attacked by a dog or an animal.
