NoiseFile turns a noisy neighbor into a complaint your city can act on. Record it. Check it against the rule. File it.

WORKS OFFLINE. NO ACCOUNT.
Open it and record. No login, no code texted to you, no internet needed. What you save stays on your phone until you share it. No ads, no analytics.

WHAT PEOPLE USE IT FOR
A neighbor who will not turn it down. Bass through the wall. Footsteps at midnight. A barking dog. A leaf blower at 7 am. A crew that starts before the city allows. Late parties, a generator, a car alarm, an HVAC unit that never stops.

RECORD AND LOG, ANYWHERE
A live decibel meter with the limit on the dial. Each incident keeps the time, length, highest and average dB, a second-by-second trace, two photos, and the loudest 10 seconds as a clip. Record the quiet room first, then the noise from the same spot; each report shows how far above the quiet it was. Hand the log to a landlord, HOA, or code enforcement.

CHECK YOUR CITY'S RULE (54 CALIFORNIA CITIES)
Pick your city. Pick what you hear: animal, general noise, or construction. You see the rule in the city's own words, the dB limit or quiet hours if the code sets them, and what the city needs before it acts. 162 rules across 54 cities, each with its code section and source link: 51 Bay Area cities from San Francisco, Oakland and San Jose to Pacifica and Santa Cruz, plus Santa Monica, Burbank and Beverly Hills. Elsewhere the meter, log and report still work; the rule check covers only the listed cities.

FILE THE COMPLAINT
Export a sealed PDF report: cover, summary, one page per incident with a chart and the city check. The app writes the complaint from your incident, then opens the city's form, email, phone line, or packet. Where the city has a web form, a guide shows every box with the answer next to it.

FREE, AND ONE UNLOCK
Picking your city, reading the rule, the meter, saving incidents, and the city check are free. One unlock, $4.99 once, opens the sealed PDF report, the form guide, and the filled-in city email. No subscription. Google Play remembers the purchase.
