NoiseFile

Privacy policy

Effective July 30, 2026

NoiseFile is a local-first Android app. The app does not create user accounts, show ads, use analytics, or send personal information to the developer.

Microphone

NoiseFile uses the microphone while the user runs the sound meter. Audio samples are processed on the device to calculate estimated sound levels. Raw audio is not saved, uploaded, or shared by NoiseFile.

Incident information

Saved incident details—including dates, durations, estimated sound levels, locations entered by the user, impacts, and notes—remain in private app storage on the device. NoiseFile excludes this incident history from Android cloud backup and device transfer. A user can remove it by clearing NoiseFile's app data or uninstalling the app.

User-directed sharing and official links

NoiseFile can prepare complaint text or an incident-history report. The app shares that text only when the user chooses Android's share command and selects a destination. NoiseFile can also open an official city website or phone dialer selected by the user. The chosen website, phone service, or sharing app handles information under its own privacy policy.

Children

NoiseFile is intended for adults handling neighborhood noise complaints and is not directed to children under 13.

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
